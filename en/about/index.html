About EuroJackys

EuroJackys is an independent European fanbase for Jackson Wang — built by fans, for fans, in French and English.

EuroJackys was founded on 14 August 2025, in the middle of the Magic Man 2 era, with one simple idea: give Jackson Wang's European fans — the Jackys — a common home. Jackson's story is global: born in Hong Kong, debuted in South Korea, now building an international career between Asia, the United States and Europe. His European fandom deserved a space of its own.

What we do

We publish verified news about Jackson Wang and Team Wang, with one strong rule: every fact is checked against reliable sources — including Chinese-language ones — before we post it. Everything is published in French and English, side by side.

Around the news, we run recurring fan formats: the Jackyroscope (a weekly horoscope where real astrology meets fandom humour), the Jacksonland Recap (a weekly English-language recap of everything that happened), and Throwback Friday (a weekly dive into iconic Jackson moments). We also maintain a map of Jackys across Europe and a community group chat.

An independent fanbase

EuroJackys is entirely fan-run and independent. We are not affiliated with Jackson Wang, Team Wang, Team Wang Design or any of their partners. EuroJackys is also a separate project from the account known as “Team Jackson Wang Europe” — the two are not related.

Where to find us

Our official website is eurojackys.com, and EuroJackys is active on six platforms: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, X, YouTube.
