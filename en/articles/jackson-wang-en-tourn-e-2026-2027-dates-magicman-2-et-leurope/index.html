Jackson Wang Tour 2026-2027

Jackson Wang on tour: the MAGICMAN 2 World Tour

Are there any European dates?

No. Not yet. We know, that's the answer nobody wanted to read.

But here's what's true: in an interview given in May 2026, Jackson explicitly named Europe among the regions planned for the rest of the tour, alongside Argentina, China and Australia. The tour is officially branded 2025-2027. So there's plenty of road left.

So no, nothing is announced. But no, it isn't over either.

And the day it drops, we'll probably be the first ones screaming.

Confirmed dates

Phase 1 — Asia (2025) Opened on 3 and 4 October 2025 in Bangkok, then Macao, Jakarta, Kuala Lumpur, Manila and Tokyo.

Phase 2 — Americas (spring 2026) Started 31 March 2026 at the Kia Forum in Los Angeles, followed by Oakland, Vancouver, Rosemont, Brooklyn and Toronto. Then Latin America, wrapped in May 2026.

Phase 3 — Mainland China (autumn 2026 → early 2027)
